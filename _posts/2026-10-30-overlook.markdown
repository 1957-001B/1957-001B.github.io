written at my leisure. what follows is what i chose to keep. the rest of the talk stays off this page.

from a dark hill a town is a line of bulbs. it is easy to call that nothing. existing for its own sake. then to make a ledger: almost everyone net zero, a few net negative, a remainder so small it barely has a name, and that remainder more self-reliant than the rest.

the concentration is real. new surplus that compounds comes from few hands. a lot of paid hours are a queue tending a queue. that part i will not take back.

net zero is still the wrong name for the base. the grid, the flour, the night shift, the person who keeps a machine from failing: delete them and the small remainder spends its surplus learning how to eat. more able is not more independent. a transfer is not the same act as a rent. care aimed at a body is not overhead aimed at a broken process. the ledger only stays that clean if those are one job.

an overlook cannot sort the windows. waste and a life use the same bulbs.

the other mistake is welding a full life to one hard hour. a market, a loaf, a drive that ends: these cannot prove you are the remainder, and they cannot prove you are not. they are the condition. the work, if it is real, is still there on the other side of them. greatness bolted on top is what makes the hour feel like a verdict. take the verdict off.
